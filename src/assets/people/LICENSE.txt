worker.glb: third-party assets
================================

worker.glb is built from two packs by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com):

- Universal Base Characters [Standard]
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
  Used: the male base body (Superhero_Male_FullBody) with its eyes and eyebrows, and the hair meshes
  Hair_Beard, Hair_SimpleParted and Hair_Buzzed (rigged to the head bone).

- Universal Animation Library [Standard]
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
  Used: the clips Idle_Loop, Idle_Talking_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Interact, PickUp_Table,
  Sitting_Enter, Sitting_Idle_Loop, Sitting_Talking_Loop, Sitting_Exit and Dance_Loop, copied onto the
  male skeleton.
